# assets/img

Screenshots of the pastor's dashboard: a Portuguese set (`painel-*`) used on `igrejas.html` and an English set (`dashboard-*`) used on `en/churches.html`. `faces/` holds the six demo portraits (AI-generated, nobody real) at 168 px, used at 56 px on the churches pages' demo cards; the originals are in `demo/src/faces/`.

## Pulse and guide screens (both sets), captured 2026-10-13/14

- `painel-pulso` (1433×1330), `painel-sermao-celula` (1433×1300), `dashboard-pulse` (1433×1148) and `dashboard-sermon-group` (1433×1300) were captured by Rodrigo with the FireShot browser extension on the **perto-sim** deployment's dashboard: Igreja Vida Nova (BRVN26) for the Portuguese set, Grace Community Church (CAGR26, locale `en`) for the English set. Simulation view, light theme, product commit `3575dea`, the week of 2026-10-05 pulse, captured 2026-10-13/14.
- The pulses are cropped just after the gratitude and suggestion cards, so both now show the reasons for gratitude and the suggested passages in frame; the guides are cropped after the two buttons ("Gerar outra versão" / "Exportar Markdown" and their English twins). All four are 1433 px wide.
- The sim's strip sentence "Simulação — dados sintéticos. Nenhuma pessoa real." / "Simulation — synthetic data. No real people." and the ⚗️ badges are kept in frame on purpose and must never be cropped out (see the product's `docs/PHASE-2-SIMULATION-DESIGN.md` §3.4). Every row is a simulated persona.
- WebP (quality 88) is served first, PNG (optimized with Pillow) is the fallback. To regenerate: open the sim dashboard from the sim console ("Open Sim dashboard"), pick the light theme, visit `#/pulse` and `#/guides`, capture the full page with FireShot, crop at the same points, keep the file names and the 1433 px width, and update the `width`/`height` on the `<img>` tags.

## Care queue screens (unchanged)

- `painel-fila-cuidado` (1328×800): the **perto-sim** deployment's dashboard (Igreja Vida Nova, BRVN26), Simulation view, light theme, product commit `1f0c0a5` on `develop`, captured 2026-10-08 (evening, ET), showing the "Em andamento" filter. Viewport slices at 1327 CSS px wide (1x), stitched into one page image and cropped at the bottom.
- `dashboard-care-queue` (1328×1120): the **perto-qa** deployment's dashboard, Grace Community Church (CAGR26, locale `en`) demo congregation, Simulation view (seeded synthetic data), light theme, product commit `21f4c75` on `develop`, captured 2026-10-09 (morning, ET). On this deployment the Simulation view marks itself with the "Live | Simulation" view toggle ("Simulation" selected) and the ⚗️ badges, one beside the toggle, one beside the page title and one on every row, rather than the sim deployment's strip sentence; the toggle and the badges are kept in frame on purpose and must never be cropped out. It shows the default "All (4)" filter, one new, two in progress and one closed request, and ends after the monthly-report line. Viewport slices at 1326 CSS px wide, stitched, cropped at the bottom, then one pixel of each edge replicated so the file keeps the 1328 px width.
- Every row is a synthetic persona on both deployments. WebP (quality 88) is served first, PNG is the fallback. To regenerate: open the dashboard with the Simulation view, pick the light theme, visit `#/care`, capture, and keep the same file names and widths (1328 px).
