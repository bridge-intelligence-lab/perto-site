For churches that want to be near during the week.

Perto is in pilot with a few churches, chosen one at a time. We are looking for design-partner churches: communities that use Perto with their members, tell us what works and what does not, and help decide what comes next. It costs nothing during the pilot.

What the church receives

  • A WhatsApp number of your church's own, with the church's name and photo, that members add with a link or QR.
  • Pastoral company every night, in your tradition's voice, with verified verses. And a word in the morning, inside the conversation the member opened.
  • A dashboard for the pastor: the week's pulse (the themes weighing on people, without names, only at 25 or more permissions), the care queue (who asked to be called, with a first name and a sentence if they chose), and a small-group guide generated from the sermon's audio.
  • A weekly card with a verse, in the church's name, that a member can send to a friend. The friend comes in through the same door.

What we ask

  1. A pastor or leader as the point of contact, on WhatsApp, who receives the dashboard and answers the care queue within a few days.
  2. That the church introduces Perto to its members in its own way: in the service, the group chat, the bulletin. We prepare the material.
  3. One conversation a month, half an hour, to hear what you saw. What you say changes the product.

What Perto does not do

It does not replace the pastor, does not do clinical counselling, never messages someone who has not written first, does not read conversations for the church and sells nothing to members. In a crisis it gives the 988 line and offers to tell the pastoral team, with the person's YES. The privacy policy says the rest, in plain words.
