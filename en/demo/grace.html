How it works

  1. You send a message in the evening, text or voice, however you are.
  2. Perto answers in seconds: a short pastoral word and a verse checked word for word against the Bible. Never a verse alone on a hard night.
  3. In the morning, at the hour you chose, a word for the day arrives, tied to what you brought the night before.
  4. What you write is yours. Nobody at the church reads it. DELETE removes everything, PAUSE goes quiet.
